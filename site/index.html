WHY THIS AI EXISTS

Ethics before expansion.

A person-centred AI must also be independently testable, accountable and open to correction.

01

Human authorship

B.E.N. supports creativity through instruction, discussion, critique and connections. Humans choose, practise and make the creative work.

02

No algorithmic rulers

The AI may explain and suggest, never silently vote, enforce punishment, approve funding or claim powers held by people.

03

No social credit

No ranking people by money, kindness, politics, productivity or conformity. Voluntary, private appreciation is not a privilege score.

04

Privacy by default

Data minimisation, explicit consent, independent safeguarding and member control. Personal stories aren't AI training material by default.

05

Transparent provenance

Disclose which model is running, which sources were used, what isn't verified and the limits of licensing or expertise.

06

Be independently challengeable

Open development, published evaluations, human appeals, model portability, accountability and clear exit routes.

The full ethics and open-source documents

The repository includes docs/AI_ETHICS_CHARTER.md, docs/CREATIVE_EDUCATION_CHARTER.md and docs/OPEN_SOURCE_STRATEGY.md. The ethics charter is a proposal until formally approved.

A public manifesto cannot substitute for an actual policy, technical tests, trained humans, or the right to challenge a harmful outcome.
